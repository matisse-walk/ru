# Монпарнас Матисса: русская версия

Russian (kids') edition of **Matisse's Montparnasse**, a walk through the Paris of Henri Matisse and his friends, made for the MATISSE project meeting in Paris, 21 October 2026. Live at <https://matisse-walk.github.io/ru/>. The English guide is at <https://matisse-walk.github.io/>.

Same app as the English release (a MapLibre map over OpenFreeMap tiles, stickers for the stops, the camera over the comic strip, the "look up" flip to the real artwork, the sticker album, the dinner quiz), with:

- all texts rewritten in Russian for children of 8–12 (second person «ты», short sentences, the same verified facts, with the Russian links added: Мари Васильева, Щукин, the Hermitage);
- Cyrillic fonts (Playfair Display, Source Sans 3, Neucha);
- eight audio episodes generated in Russian with NotebookLM from the same fact-checked notes (`audio/`);
- the same eight comic pages (`comic/`, drawn in paper-cut style with Gemini, captionless; the captions are Russian HTML) and the same public-domain artworks (`art/`, licences in `art/credits.json`).

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `index.html`, `app.js`, `app.css`, `content.js` | the app; `content.js` and the interface strings are generated by `tools/build_ru.py` |
| `tools/ru_texts.py` | all Russian texts: stations, 35 map cards, quiz |
| `tools/build_ru.py` | merges them onto the English files kept in `tools/en/` |
| `dinner.html`, `credits.html` | the quiz and the credits |
| `audio/` | `ep-00.mp3` … `ep-07.mp3` (Russian) |

Rebuild after editing texts: `python tools/build_ru.py`.
